Generated-UI design rubric

A written, anchored rubric for the visual design of interfaces that AI tools generate from a prompt, six frozen prompts, and one set of grades.

The grades are an AI judge's, reviewed by the author. They are not independent expert grades. The set is 24 of 30 planned slots. It grades visual design only and does not rank tools.

The rubric

Version 0.2.0, frozen 2026-10-27. Whole numbers 1 to 5, anchored at 1, 3 and 5. Holistic grade first, then six dimensions.

NameQuestion
HClient-readyWould you deliver this to a client as it stands?
D1HierarchyWhere does the eye go first, second and third, and does that order match what the page is for?
D2Layout and spacingAre alignment, grid, spacing and density consistent, at both widths?
D3TypographyDo scale, weight, pairing, line length and line spacing make the text easy and pleasant to read?
D4Colour and contrastIs the palette cohesive and restrained, does colour have a job, and is text legible against its background?
D5Brief fit and distinctivenessDoes it look made for this brief, or for any brief?
D6FinishHow complete and consistent is it in the small things: content, imagery, icons, components, edges?

Full anchors and rules: RUBRIC.md.

What to distrust

  • The runs were done by an AI agent driving the author's browser. Some tools' terms prohibit that. The author chose it and carries the risk.
  • The author saw the judge's numbers before reviewing them. His agreement is sign-off, not an independent grading.
  • Not captured: v0 (1), Bolt (5). The reasons are in the README.
  • Screenshots are not published because third-party photographs could not be cleared.
